Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about university admissions

How do I check my eligibility for universities?

Once you create an account and enter your SSC/HSC GPA and academic group, your dashboard will automatically show you all programs you're eligible for. You can also use the "Refresh Eligibility" button on the dashboard to update the list.

How do I bookmark a university?

On any university card, click the bookmark icon (♡) to save it. Your bookmarked universities will appear in your Dashboard under "My Bookmarks".

Is there any fee to use this platform?

No, the platform is completely free for students. We do not charge any fees for checking eligibility, comparing universities, or accessing information.

What documents do I need for admission?

Typically you'll need:

  • SSC certificate and marksheet
  • HSC certificate and marksheet (or equivalent)
  • Recent passport‑size photograph
  • National ID or birth certificate
  • Application fee payment receipt (if required by the university)
Some universities may ask for additional documents – check the specific program details.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page, enter your email, and we'll send you a password reset link. The link expires in 1 hour for security.

Is my personal information secure?

Absolutely. We use industry‑standard encryption to protect your data. Your information is never shared with third parties without your explicit consent. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.

How can I contact support?

You can reach us via email at support@admissionhelper.bd, call us at +880 1234-567890 (Sun–Thu, 9 AM – 6 PM), or use the Contact Form. We typically reply within 24 hours.
